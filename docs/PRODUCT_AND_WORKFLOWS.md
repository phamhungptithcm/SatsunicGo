# Product and current workflows

Single-business US/JP/KR buying assistance; Vietnamese default, VND collections, source currency retained. Human staff purchase and update shipping. AI prepares reviewed drafts and cannot authorize money or shipping.

Customer has two distinct purchase flows (approved PURCHASE-FLOW-018 v1):

- Listed products: browse the paginated published catalog → select explicitly configured orderable product, supported variant and quantity → Google authentication → confirm all-inclusive listed VND total → transactional catalog checkout → full verified payment → staff buying → receiving/packing → shipping/tracking → customer explicitly confirms receipt. No staff quote, deposit or second payment. Server freezes product/version/terms/price snapshot, never trusts client totals, and replays the same checkout operation on retry. Historical referencePrice is not a listedPrice and cannot authorize a collection. Products without valid explicit listed pricing remain unavailable for direct ordering.
- Custom purchase requests (products absent from listing): bounded multi-line request → staff review and versioned quote → customer acceptance → first payment (current implementation: 50%) → buying → receiving/packing → approved final total → second payment for outstanding amount → shipping/tracking → customer explicitly confirms receipt. Historical orders without purchaseKind retain this custom policy.

Pending transfer notices, QR/link creation, redirects and chat answers do not confirm funds. Staff buying and dispatch use verified available funds after refunds/reservations. Catalog customer liability cannot be raised by freight finalization or consolidation; internal freight remains operational accounting. Existing customer-approved cancellation/return proposals can reduce liability while retaining the original immutable catalog snapshot and financial history. Request drafts survive login; catalog uncertain checkout identity survives page reload within the same tab. CSV, reorder (catalog returns to current listing checkout), order export, profile/address, membership invoices/history, support thread and notifications remain separate features.

AI readers distinguish full catalog payment from custom installments. Published product references link to product details/checkout; sourced catalog answers suppress custom shopping drafts. Bounded AI search is not an exhaustive catalog search and does not establish absence. In-chat catalog checkout requires an explicit supported variant/quantity and customer confirmation; its durable operation can be reconciled after response loss. Recipient details are saved privately before opening payment and are absent from AI turns. AI cannot purchase autonomously or confirm funds.

Staff: current-role commands and queues for quotes, linewise buying/receiving, changes, holds, parcels/consolidation, manual final freight and dispatch, finance review, membership plans/invoices, CRM notes/follow-up, bounded UTC dashboard, staff access, pricing, products/posts/media and campaign drafts. Customer projections exclude internal manifests, purchase evidence and private notes.

Manual steps are explicit: product/source verification, purchase evidence, transfer reconciliation, carrier tracking, caption copying. AI, SMTP, payOS and public launch remain disabled/unverified until external configuration and acceptance. Full return-after-delivery warehouse inspection, operational refund/reversal and dead-letter resolution, broader English coverage and complete master workflow acceptance remain open. This document describes current source, not a completion certificate.
