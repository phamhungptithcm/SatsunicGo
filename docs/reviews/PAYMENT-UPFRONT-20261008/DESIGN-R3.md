## User steering and flow

Keep the existing purchase-request form; change its final action to **Thêm vào giỏ hàng** and label custom items **Cần tìm mua**. The three steps remain Món hàng → Thông tin thêm → Kiểm tra. Add-to-cart never promises an order, payment or assigned staff task. The existing multi-item/image/draft capabilities must survive implementation.

Catalog addition and custom addition enter the same cart. Checkout selects lines → delivery details → review/consent → one initial payment. Backend preserves separate child-order contracts and exact allocations. Staff sees paid custom items in existing Workbench, checks source/options/price and asks the customer before exceeding approved spend. Catalog fixed prices never inherit custom top-ups. Receipt group lists both kinds without double-counting ledger revenue.
